Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 16, 2026 · Developer: Kraig Wastlund

Boing... does not collect any data. There are no analytics, no ads, no tracking and no accounts, and nothing about you or how you play is sent to the developer or to anyone else.

Data on your device

Your game progress, best scores and settings are stored only on your device. Deleting the app removes them.

Photos

Boing... asks for permission to add to your photo library only when you choose Save Image from the share sheet to save your score card. It never reads your photos.

Children

Because Boing... collects no data, it collects no data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

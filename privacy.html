Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026 · Developer: Kraig Wastlund

Boing... collects only anonymous crash and diagnostic data, to find and fix bugs, and the feedback you choose to send. There are no analytics, no ads, no tracking and no accounts, and nothing that identifies you is collected. You can turn crash reporting off at any time in the app.

Crash and diagnostic data

Boing... uses Firebase Crashlytics (a Google service) to send the developer a report when the app crashes or hits an error. A report contains:

  • technical details of the crash or error (where in the app's code it happened);
  • your device model, iOS version and the app's version;
  • a short log of recent game events, such as a round starting or ending, a bomb going off, or a purchase completing or failing;
  • game state at the time, such as the round, the number of balls, your best round and best shot, the equipped ball skin, and whether sounds and vibrations are on;
  • the app’s language and your device’s region setting (for example “es_MX”);
  • a random install ID created by the app, used only to group reports from the same installation. It is not linked to your name, email, Apple ID or any other account, and deleting the app discards it.

Reports never include your name, email, contacts, photos, location or anything you type. The data is not used for advertising or tracking and is not sold. Google processes it on the developer's behalf under the Firebase privacy terms.

Turning it off: open Settings in Boing... and switch Crash Reports off. No more reports or diagnostic data are sent from then on.

Feedback you send

If you use Send Feedback in Settings, Boing... sends the developer your message together with: the app version and build, your device model and iOS version, the app’s language and region, the equipped ball skin, your best round and best shot, the current round, and (only while Crash Reports is on) the same short log of recent game events described above. Email addresses and anything that looks like a password or key are removed from your message before it is sent. Nothing is sent unless you tap Send, so please don’t include personal information in your message.

Feedback is delivered through Firebase Cloud Functions (a Google service) and stored as an issue in the developer’s private issue tracker on GitHub, used only to fix bugs and improve the game. A random ID created by the app is used only to limit how often feedback can be sent; it is not stored with your message. To have feedback you sent deleted, email the address below.

Data on your device

Your game progress, best scores and settings are stored only on your device. Deleting the app removes them.

Photos

Boing... asks for permission to add to your photo library only when you choose Save Image from the share sheet to save your score card. It never reads your photos.

Children

Boing... does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. The anonymous crash and diagnostic data described above contains nothing that identifies a person.

Changes

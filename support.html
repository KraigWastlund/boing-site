Support

How do I aim?

Touch the board, drag back, and watch the preview dots show your shot. Let go to launch your balls. Each brick shows how many hits it has left.

How do bombs work?

Pink bomb bricks explode when they break, spraying bomb balls across the board, and each one adds a ball to your launcher. Clear the whole board to earn header bombs. Tap the bomb button at the top right to arm your next shot, or tap it while your ball is flying to set it off right where the ball is.

Can I turn off sounds or vibrations?

Yes. Tap the gear at the top left to open Settings, where Sounds, Vibrations and Stats for Nerds each have their own switch.

Will I lose my game if I close the app?

No. Your game is saved automatically, so you can pick up where you left off.
